# Notices

## Data sources

This project downloads public files at run time from NSE (nseindia.com and
nsearchives.nseindia.com), AMFI (portal.amfiindia.com), the US SEC (sec.gov),
mfapi.in and Yahoo Finance. It does not redistribute them. Each source has its
own terms of use; you are responsible for complying with them, including any
limits on automated access.

Regulatory filings and exchange files are the work of the companies and
institutions that publish them. Figures shown by these tools are taken from
those sources or computed from them, and are not endorsed by them.
